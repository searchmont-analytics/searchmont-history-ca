GLENDALE TRUCKS DAILY HAUL ABOUT 140 MILES Sault Starjan 31 1931 Snow Shovelled on Road Where Sieigh Haul Used Glendale, Jan.29.-Becauseof the bad weather Sunday, the Glendale Bugs did not play Searchmont on their new rink. Wehope to send a good delegation to the Searchmont hockey team dance Friday. The trucks used by the Frair-Rath Lumber Co., tohaul some of their telephone poles have been more successful than anticipated. The trucks are now making six and seven trips aday. They cover from 120 to140 miles a day and carry a load half of these distances. The loading of railroad cars with poles isto be resumed at once and is to continue steadily. The Eastern Lumber Co., operating on Ranger Lakewith headquarters at Glendale, have now quit cutting logs and have started their sleigh haul. They have been forced to show el snow onto their roads and sprinkle it with water, making iced roads. The reason for this extra work is the lack of snow in this vicinity. Manyold timers hereabouts state they have never seen sucn a winter.
