COMPLETE PHONE LINE THIS WEEK Sault Star Apr 23 1934 Provides Complete Forestry Branch Hookup in This Area Construe tion of the new forestry branch telephone line from Island Lake to Glendale will be completed by the end of this week, Chief Forest er Nigel Kensit told the Star in an interview today. Necessary poles, brackets and insulatore are all up and the stringing of the line isabout half completed. Work is being done by members of the forestry branch staff. The new telephone line will complete the forestry branch circuit. Present lines extend from the Sault to Batch awan a, from Blind River-to Glendale via camp 18 and Ranger Lake. The Ieland-Lake Glendale stretch completes the hookup which takes in all headquarters and all towers.
