Searchmont Resort Is Success Story Searchmont Valley Ski Club is beginning to look more like the beau tif ui dream Ian McDonald and Herb Lederer have hung on to ever since they took an option on the Algoma mountain five or six years ago. The pace quickened when the project became a company two years back and really picked up speed over the past summer. With its altogether new look, the place begins to resemble the ski resorts you see in Ski magazine, It's still util it aran. The emphasis remains on family fun, promotion of young skiers and healthful winter exercise. While the latest in ski togs and equipment is becoming common, it still is no status symbol at Searchmont. You rate if you enjoy yourself, are thoughtful of others on the hill, and are a genial companion for after-ski snacks in the new lodge (with inside plumbing, yet!) Therows of cars lining up behind the lodge and the steady stream of traffic over the 35 mile road from the Sault bringing spectators are proof that Searchmont is beginning to rate. Searchmont has had backing from a number of influential Sooites since the company was formed and most of them are using the resort as a form of family activity. The first of many family chalets has been erected as a joint project by the Dr. Elgies and the Bill Adamses for a weekend residence. Special fares for bus and train passengers have been arranged by ACR and Steel City Coaches. And best of all from the skiers' standpoint is tr.e new poma lift which whisks them nearly to the top of the mountain. The most enterprising then have more than a mile of splendid downhill runs. Ski Trails Searchmont Slopes Attract All Ages By DOROTHY OLAR Sault Star Correspondent SEARCHMONT-Fresh packed snow has improved condition on the slopes of Searchmont Valley Ski Resort. Condi tions were ideal Wednesday and the fresh snow and beautiful weather brought skiing fans out in car loads. Mr. and Mrs. Henry Bullock, a husband and wife team from the Sault, make a very handsome pair- Henry in a dark outfit and Mary in avery smart Scandinavian pattern sweater and black slacks, The Herb Lederer's were out in force. Daughter Jackie, aged five, and Herbie Jr., three years-old, are doing very well on skis. Wee Robert aged two says "Daddy, I want skis too," SO probably before long he'll be joining the rest of the family on the slopes. Echo Bay Mrs. Wilfred Everett of Little Current spent the weekend with her parents, Mr.and Mrs. Sam Graham. Beginners hill is a real hive ofaetivity -beginner doesn't mean juniors only -we have them here from six to 60or more accurately-from three to65-with one thing in common, the first pair of skis. Herb Lederer is the capable instructor and is kept quite busy weekends and Wednesdays but as Herb says There is always room for one more. So let me issue an open invitation to anyone with a hankering to ski-don't put it off any longer, come out and join the fun. As the slogan says-don't wait till spring -do it now. So nice to see familiar faces in the crowd. Last Sunday we noticed Lloyd Sundstrom among the spectators. Lloyd is a former home towner having spent many years here when his family owned the Searchmont Hotel. Two sisters, Gertie, Ewan and Mabel Montgomery,(hope I'm right there) Mabel's husband Bob and Johnny Johnston made up the party. Fresh fallen snow assures a good weekend for skiing so folks "let's rally round the Pama-lift."

Ski Club Opens At Searchmont The Searchmont Valley Ski Club opens its new hill to the public Sunday. Ian MacDonald and Herb Led erer, who developed the location over the past summer, announced that the road to the lodge has been plowed clear of snow, and that a snack bar will be operated in the heated lodge. The hill can be seen on the right hand side of the road, just before entering Searchmont. Thr road into the hill begins just after the Searchmont General store. People that have seen the hill so far have been very pleased with its size and adaptability to all classes of skiers. Snow conditions on the two developed runs couldn't be better. There are steep slopes and gentle slopes, and a slalom run that stands strictly for the expert. The hill was previously packed well, only partway. But yesterday the boys worked hard packing the top part of the slope to comfortable wi td th, to suit the intermediate skier. Groups of skiers have been out to the hill on the past few weekends, and an excellent crowd is expected to attend the opening of the location Sunday. The lodge is heated now, after Herb and Ian brought two stoves into the log cabin lodge, Friday. Sault Star Jan 25 1958 IAN MacDONALD ..work completed
