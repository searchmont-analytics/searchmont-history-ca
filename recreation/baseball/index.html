Autio D eyes lift record in Saturday competition BY ALEX MITCHEL I Of The Star Bill Autio hopes to begin a new victory streak Saturday when defending champion Diodati'sPowerliftingClubof Sault Ste. Marie hosts the Central Canadian Powerlifting championships. The 32-year-old Autio, who has a string of six consecutive victories in the 242-pound class from meets dating back to November.1974, will enter the 220-pound category for Saturday's competitions. Normally, the steel worker would have been considered a favorite in the 242-pound category, but when he contacted a severe stomach flu infection in April, Autio lost 25 pounds, During the sum mer he was only able to gain seven pounds leaving him around 220. "I feel strong, but I have lost a little,"he admitted Tuesday. But by dropping down a category, Autio is well within reach of establishing a total lift record in the 220-pound category. Only one lifter has passed 1400-pounds in Canada. Autio did 1440-pounds at the Eastern Canadian championships Jan.12 in Quebec City. including 625 in the dead-lift.
