Whitman Dam

Whitman DamDam Construction

Whitman Dam with log chute on right circa 1920s

Whitman Dam was located east of Searchmont on the Goulais river. It was built in the early 1920s and by the 1960s the dam no longer existed.

Whitman Dam showing log chute circa 1920s. The Goulais river floods and overtops the Whitman Dam and floods the valley below in Searchmont. The final sequence is located just south of the present day (2026) Goulais river road bridge. The film shows a vehicle stuck in the flooded road and a horse drawn wagon coming to the rescue. The building in the background is Tessier's store in Searchmont. This is a National Film Board of Canada production
