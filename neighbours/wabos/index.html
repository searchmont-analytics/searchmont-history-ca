Vihtori Kallio receives Finnish consul medal Mar 1 1973 Sault Star Photo VIHTORI KALLIORECEIVED FINNISH PIONEER MEDAL with Finnish consut. Mauno Kaihle (left).and Else Kallio. Vihtori Kallio, who came to Canada in 1909 at the age of 19, was presented with a Pioneer Medal of Honor on behalf of the Finnish Historical Soci ely of Sudbury. This organization which includes Finnish people of Sault Ste. Marie, currently is compiling a history of Finnish people in Canada.lt is expected to be completed next year. Presentation of the medal was made to Mr. Kallio Wednesday night in a program at St. Mary's Finnish Lutheran Church. Mr. Kallio did bush work out of Searchmont. He haswo.children, Mrs. William Knuuttila(Aune)with home he lives here and a son, Walter of Searchmont. The program, in Finnish, included selections by St. Mary's church choir directed by Oliver Turpela and a group from the Finnish Pentecostal Betania church choir. Maria Hill gave a reading from the Kalevala.the Finnish national epos. Erkki Jokinen and Vilho Hyykylainen sang solos and Mr. Turpela performed on the organ.
