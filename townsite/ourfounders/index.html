The Story of Searchmont begins with Francis Hector Clergue Clergue was born in Maine and later became a businessman and entrepreneur who arrived in Sault Ste, Mariein 1894. Hecreated an industrial empire in Sault Ste. Marie and Algoma, that included a Steel Manufacturing Company, HydroElectric Power Company, Pulp and Paper Company, and Algoma Central&Hudson Bay Railway. The town of Goulais(later named Searchmont) was built asa model townsite complete with a park, which Clergue hoped would be usedto build other settlements along the railway right of way. A sawmill was built on the banks of the Goulais River, and charcoal kilns were built near the base of the existing Ski Resort. The sawmill provided lumber to build the settlement, and the charcoal kilns provided charcoal which was used in the steelmaking process.

Searchmont was named in honor of Theodore C. Search (1841-1920) Mr. Search served in various positions at the Consolidated Lake Superior Company(The parent company of the Lake Superior Pulp and Paper Company)which hada presence in Searchmont. Mr. Search served as Secretary Treasurer, VicePresident and Director. As the story goes, Francis H. Clergue who founded the Consolidated Lake Superior Company brought Search to the area on the railway and told Mr.Search that if he could secure money from investors for his Company the town would be named in his honor. This happened and the town became known as Searchmont. Mr. Search was aren owned American businessman who was the president of the National Association of Manufacturers, a founder of Philadelphia University and managing director of the Stetson Hat Company.
