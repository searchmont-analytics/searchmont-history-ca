New Building Is Booming SEARCHMONT-Construction valued at thousands of dollars is underway in a building boom which has reached this area lately. The largest project is the new school on which work is progressing at a favorable rate. The aluminium building has plenty of large windows which face Highway 556. Don Gratton is just putting the finishing touches to a new two-storey chicken house in which he will house his large flock of birds. Additions are being made to the Allan Roberts and Oscar Boyer homes and the materials for Larry Wagner's new home have been delivered to the site Construction is expected to get started soon. The Searchmont Hotel has built an addition designated·for office space and Mr.and Mrs. K. Leed and family have moved into their new home on the road behind the mill. PERSONALS Ernie Johnson is able to be out again after a severe case of hepatitis. Mrs. Wagner was able to leave hospital and is convalescing at the home of her daughter in the Sault. Alex Olar is confined to bed with a back injury. Mr. Olar is much improved but the doctor has another week.
