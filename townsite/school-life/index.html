A red school house trimmed with spotless white? Or a little shack of withered logs backed by a clear blue sky? ranks. The color scheme is not important-it was a stronghold of a civilization in a primitive land. Within its walls devoted men and women dispensed a heritage of learning. Under its roof, cherished traditions from scores of countries mingled and merged. It was the cradle of a nation growing up. The pioneers built it with their own hands, by the sweat of their brows, from timbers mont. meant to make their own. 119 Schoolmates stand in for Grade 5-6 class as students ride tour train through S earchmont Faithful class sees own sign from tour train BY NILA TRUMBLE Special To The Star SEARCHMONT-Itstill goesoneverymorningatg o'clock sharp in Searchmont. As the Agawa Canyon tour train rolls through the town, the passengers are greeted by Glen Atkinson's Grade 5-6 class from the Searchmont public school, toting signs and waving "Good Morning, drop us a line. The ritual has been going on for four years. On Friday morning, Oct. 11, the5-6class was not there to wave. Instead, the entire school was emptied of students and teachers onto the highway in front of the school, along with parents and friends, as the tour train made its run through Searchmont. On this day, Mr. Atkin- son and his class were passengers on the train, courtesy of the ACR and previous fans who had waved back from the train on their way to the canyon. A'free breakfast and lunch were served to the students on the train and at 4:30 p.m.they were dropped off at Searchmont, a stop not usually on the train's tour schedule.

Searchmont youngsters provided warm greetings to Trudeau family in September thanked by. Trudeau Hospitable students SEARCHMONT-Pierreand The Kids said thanks to Glenn Atkinson's Grades5and6class here Monday at Searchmont central public school. Responding to hospitality given his family during an area visit Sept. 25, Prime Minister Trudeau wrote his appreciation ina letter to Searchmont-area students. The letter, which arrived at the school Monday morning, thanked the students for their "warm and special' welcome while the the Trudeau family was passing through Searchmont during the visitors’Algo- ma Central Railway(ACR)fall color tour trip to Agawa Canyon. The Prime Minister also expressed his appreciation for the stories and smelly stickers the students gave to his sons. "They (his sons) liked the pizza-flavored smelly stickers, plus the licorice and banana cream pie (stickers),'he wrote.“we also enjoyed the stories and paintings very much. You all deserve an A for effort." Mr. Atkinson, whose students have received more than100 letters from ACR train riders after being welcomed through Searchmont with a‘Good Morning' sign, said his class was site. wondering if they would receive a response from the Prime Minister. "We're really excited the letter was personalized. It wasn't a form letter,"he said.“I told the class it'sreallysomething. Sept. 26. Mr. Trudeau concluded his letter by telling the students he and his sons‘treasured your welcome and kind words.'
